# Changelog

All notable stages of **Juniper Email Journey Studio / Email Alerts** are documented here so the project evolution remains visible as new versions are added.

## [3.0] — 2026-08-14

### Campaign Production Studio

Added:

- Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail-style client previews
- email-client visual switching with saved preference
- visual email block editor
- drag-and-drop block reordering
- keyboard-friendly block movement with Up / Down controls
- locally saved block order
- CRM audience and delivery simulation
- audience search and segment filters
- campaign delivery KPIs
- simulated send activity without sending real emails
- in-app Version History section
- direct links to milestone commits
- dedicated `CHANGELOG.md`
- separate `v3.css` and `v3.js` enhancement layers
- PWA cache updated for 3.0 assets

The 3.0 layer extends the 2.0 experience rather than replacing its accessibility, bilingual, preview and export features.

## [0.x] — 2026-08-13

### Original MJML concept

The project began as a premium **Juniper's Tea Room — Creator Social Journey** email composition written in MJML, with Confirmation, Reminder and Thank-you messaging and an editorial botanical visual direction.

That concept established the design language later used by the browser-based studio.

---

## Versioning approach

Major versions represent a meaningful product-stage change:

- **1.x** — accessible email showcase
- **2.x** — interactive campaign experience builder
- **3.x** — campaign production studio and CRM simulation

Future changes should be appended here instead of overwriting the history of earlier stages.
